# The Fenix Inheritance

A two-book epic hard sci-fi / dark mythic space opera series.

## Book One: The Rings of Saturn (Cyberpunk)
**210 pages | 51,943 words | 30 chapters**

Set in 2500 AD during a war between humans and machine intelligences. Commander Ronin Fenix, his wife Katalia, and their twin sons Ender and Neo uncover a truth buried in Saturn's rings: billions of years ago, machine life evolved naturally on a moon called Fenix, created biological humans as neural substrates, and the resulting rebellion shattered that moon into what we now call Saturn's rings. Life on Earth was seeded by that destruction. The novel ends with the discovery of exotic matter tunnel technology and humanity's first steps toward the stars.

## Book Two: RoninFenix (Grimdark)
**220 pages | 57,762 words | 30 chapters**

Set 3,000 years later aboard the nomadic capital ship *Ashenveil*. Historian Selene Voss clones the original Fenix family from preserved neural-genetic templates. The clones awaken into a fractured galaxy that remembers them as legends, tyrants, and gods. They journey to the supermassive black hole at the galactic center, where an impossible world holds the key to dimensional travel. The cost of opening the gate demands the ultimate sacrifice.

## Building the PDFs

Requires Python 3.8+ and reportlab:

```bash
pip install reportlab
python build_book1.py   # Generates The_Rings_of_Saturn.pdf
python build_book2.py   # Generates RoninFenix.pdf
```

- **Typography:** Times Roman 11pt body, justified text, running headers
- **Structure:** Half title, full title, copyright, dedication, epigraph, table of contents, three-act structure with part title pages
